## SHADOW

SHADOW means:

LOGICAL_NO_EXECUTION_STATE

It is not a claim of physical time travel.

The expression

BILLIONS_OF_YEARS -> 0ms

is used only as a logical-state metaphor:

an arbitrarily large conceptual interval may exist between two active
states while the transition operation itself remains computationally small.

Measured wall-clock time remains a separate dimension.
