# Sources

## Core evidence

### Ayuso-Fernandez et al. (2019)

Peroxidase evolution in white-rot fungi follows wood lignin evolution in plants.

PNAS.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6731631/

Why it matters: ancestral fungal peroxidases were reconstructed and experimentally tested against lignin substrates.

---

### Nelsen et al. (2016)

Delayed fungal evolution did not cause the Paleozoic peak in coal production.

PNAS.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4780611/

Why it matters: cautions against a simplistic single-timing explanation for lignin decay and Carboniferous coal accumulation.

---

### Janusz et al. (2017 review context)

Fungal biodegradation and enzymatic modification of lignin.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3180040/

Why it matters: describes the broader ligninolytic enzyme toolkit, including laccases, LiP, MnP, VP and accessory systems.

---

### Renault et al. (2017)

A phenol-enriched cuticle is ancestral to lignin evolution in land plants.

Nature Communications.

https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms14713

Why it matters: shows that components of phenolic metabolism predate canonical lignin and helps separate precursor chemistry from later lignified vascular structures.
